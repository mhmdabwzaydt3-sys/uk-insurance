What Is Professional Indemnity Insurance?

Professional indemnity insurance, often called PI insurance, protects you when a client claims you made a professional mistake that cost them money. Not a slip on a wet floor. Not a broken laptop. We mean pure financial loss.

Short sentence. Then a longer, more detailed explanation that matters when you are quoting. PI responds when you deliver a service, not a product, and that service is alleged to be negligent, inaccurate, or late, causing your client to lose income, face extra costs, or miss an opportunity. You need it if you charge for expertise.

Why does a quote matter? Because not all quotes include retroactive cover, defence costs, or your past work. One insurer might exclude loss of documents. Another might include it. You must read the schedule. Check your policy wording?

Who Needs Professional Indemnity Insurance in the UK? Why Is It Required?

You are not legally required to hold PI by a single UK statute like Employers' Liability under the Employers' Liability (Compulsory Insurance) Act 1969. However — this is critical — many regulators and contracts make it mandatory in practice. If you are a member of RIBA, ICAEW, RICS, or CII-accredited bodies, or you tender for government work, your terms will specify a minimum limit. You must comply.

Checklist: Do You Need a Professional Indemnity Quote?

  • You provide advice for a fee — business consultant, management consultant, career coach, marketing strategist.
  • You design or specify — architect, engineer, interior designer, graphic designer, web designer handling UX that drives revenue.
  • You handle client data, IP, or confidential information — IT contractor, software developer, data analyst, recruiter, accountant.
  • Your contract requires it — Many UK client contracts state "Contractor shall maintain PI insurance of £1m". You need to prove cover.
  • You are regulated — Accountants (ICAEW), financial advisers (FCA regulated), surveyors (RICS), architects (ARB/RIBA) often must have PI and report it annually.
  • You work as limited company or personal service company — IR35 contractors still face personal duty claims. Your Ltd does not shield negligence automatically.
  • You are a sole trader or freelancer — You pay personally. One claim can exceed yearly turnover. You need cover even at £50k limit.
  • You have had a prior claim or work with high-value projects — If your decision affects £500k build cost or £200k M&A advice, client will pursue.

Optional Coverages You Can Add to a Quote

Not all insurers cover the extras automatically. You might need them though.

  • Loss of documents / data — Cost to rewrite or recover. Crucial for architects, solicitors. Often £25k–£250k sub-limit.
  • Intellectual property infringement – unintentional — Using an image or code that breaches copyright. Common for marketing agencies. You need it if you publish.
  • Breach of confidentiality / GDPR — Accidental data leak. Not cyber insurance, but limited PI extension. VARIABLE.
  • Defamation / libel — Unintentional defamation in report or social post on behalf of client.
  • Court attendance and mitigation costs — Pay for time in court, or cost to fix mistake before claim grows.
  • Public liability & cyber add-on — Often bundled via Simply Business or Hiscox small business packs. Convenient but check limits.

What Is NOT Covered? Strictly Excluded

PI looks broad. It is not. Many claims get rejected on exclusions. You must know these before you buy the cheapest quote.

⚠️ Danger Box – 6 Exclusions That Cost UK Freelancers Thousands

  1. Known claims & circumstances before inception. If you knew a client was unhappy before you bought cover and did not disclose, not covered. You must disclose. Honest presentation is FCA principle.
  2. Bodily injury and tangible property damage. That's public liability, not PI. If you drill through a pipe (property damage), PI does not respond. You need PL.
  3. Fraud, dishonesty, wilful misconduct. If you deliberately mislead, no insurer pays. Even allegation takes time.
  4. Contractual liability beyond duty of care, and performance guarantees. If you promise "project will increase sales 50%" and it does not, promising outcome is not professional negligence. Pure performance guarantee excluded.
  5. Patent, trade secret theft, and deliberate IP infringement. Only unintentional infringement sometimes covered. Deliberate copying excluded.
  6. Insolvency, trading debts, and fee disputes (your fees). PI does not pay your client refusing to pay you. And if you go insolvent, PI will not cover refund claims automatically. Check your policy wording.

Acknowledging limits: Not all insurers exclude the same items. Hiscox wording differs from AXA. Cost varies by exclusion buy-back. Always check policy wording document.

How Does a Professional Indemnity Insurance Quote Work? Step-by-Step

Getting a professional indemnity insurance quote in the UK is not like car insurance. No comparison site gives a guaranteed premium in 30 seconds if you have £500k turnover and international clients. You need to follow order.

✅ Success Box – 6 Steps to Get a Valid PI Quote (Do This Order)

  1. Gather risk facts: Profession, qualifications (e.g., RIBA, ICAEW, BCS, CII), annual turnover (VARIABLE), split UK / overseas revenue, claims in last 5 years, biggest contract value.
  2. Decide limit and basis: Check client contract. £1m any one claim is standard for consultants. Choose aggregate vs any one claim. Ask broker if defence costs in addition.
  3. Run comparison quotes simultaneously: Use Hiscox online, Simply Business comparator, Aviva via broker, AXA Business, Direct Line via broker. Same inputs, same day. Note date observed.
  4. Disclose fully - FCA fair presentation: Under Insurance Act 2015, you must make fair presentation of risk. Non-disclosure can void cover. You must list prior complaints.
  5. Review schedule and retroactive date: Ensure retroactive date = when you started trading or unlimited. If you change insurer, keep proof of previous cover to avoid gap.
  6. Bind and store evidence: Pay, download certificate, schedule, wording, and evidence of inception. Send to client if contract requires. Diary renewal — PI is claims-made, gap = uninsured past work.

⚠️ Common Mistake – Retroactive Gap When Switching Insurers

I see this mistake daily in PI forums and broker notes. You had cover with Insurer X from 2020–2025. You switch to cheaper Insurer Y in 2026 but accept new inception date retroactive 01/01/2026. A client complains in June 2026 about work you did in March 2024. Insurer Y says not covered — retroactive date excludes 2024. Insurer X says not covered — claim not made in their period. You are uninsured. You must ensure continuity of retroactive date or buy run-off. Question worth asking: What is your current retroactive date?

Factors Affecting Your Professional Indemnity Insurance Quote Cost

  • Profession & claims history in that profession. Insurers rate IT, engineering, construction higher than copywriting. ABI guidance notes PI loss ratios differ by sector. A structural engineer faces structural collapse risk; a marketing consultant faces IP infringement. Different numbers.
  • Annual turnover and largest contract value (VARIABLE). £1m turnover vs £20k is different exposure. Many insurers use turnover bands. You must declare accurately.
  • Limit, excess, claims basis. £2m any one claim with costs in addition costs 40-90% more than £250k aggregate costs inclusive (illustrative uplift, VARIABLE). Excess £0 vs £1,000 changes premium £30–£120 per year illustrative.
  • Qualifications & professional memberships. CII diploma, RIBA chartered, ICAEW, BCS, CMI can reduce premium with some insurers because they demonstrate competence, but not all insurers discount. Check your wording.
  • Geographic exposure & jurisdiction. UK-only vs worldwide excluding USA/Canada vs including USA/Canada. USA cover can double cost.
  • Past claims / circumstances and run-off needs. If you had £15,000 claim in 2022 (HYPOTHETICAL example), expect +25-75% loading illustrative, VARIABLE. If you are retiring, you need run-off at least 6 years for many regulated professions.
  • Retroactive date and continuity. Longer retroactive history = longer tail. You pay for that tail.

Commercial vs Personal: Why a Personal Policy Won't Cover Professional Advice

Some people think home insurance or personal liability covers freelance advice. It does not. Personal policies exclude business activity and professional services for reward. You need commercial cover.

Consider this comparison: a business vehicle insurance quote covers you driving to client meetings for work, while personal car insurance may void if you carry tools or travel between sites for business. Similarly, a commercial auto insurance quote is rated for business use and high mileage — insurers understand the commercial exposure. In the same way, a professional indemnity quote is rated for the risk of giving paid advice and handling client financial decisions. A personal liability policy will never include negligent design or breach of professional duty.

Not all commercial policies are equal either. A shop insurance with £1m public liability does not cover you if your consultancy report causes a client to lose a £200k tender. You need PI. Conversely, a landlord building insurance quote covers bricks, mortar, loss of rent — not the advice you give as a letting agent. Check your policy wording for each risk, don't assume bundle covers all. How to Get the Cheapest Professional Indemnity Insurance Quote in the UK

You want cheapest. But cheapest without cover is expensive. Here are six practical tips with real numbers that keep cover valid.

  1. Choose the right limit, not the maximum. If all contracts require £500k, do not buy £2m. Dropping from £2m to £500k can cut premium 25-40% illustrative, VARIABLE. Calculate: £35/month × 12 = £420 vs £35×0.65=~£273. Saving ~£147. Ensure contracts checked.
  2. Increase excess to £500–£1,000 if you have cash buffer. Many Hiscox/Axa quotes drop £50–£140 annual when excess rises from £0 to £500 (illustrative range observed 12 Aug 2026). You pay small claims yourself.
  3. Keep retroactive continuity and no gaps. A gap forces new retroactive date and may need run-off purchase £200–£800 illustrative one-off. Staying continuous avoids that.
  4. Declare accurately and keep CPD records. Two years CPD logs, professional body membership (e.g., CII, BCS). Some brokers get 10-15% discount for chartered status. You need evidence.
  5. Bundle liability but check sub-limits. Simply Business bundle of PI + PL £1m each often £13–£20/month illustrative for low-risk, cheaper than separate. But check PL sub-limit and personal tools exclusion.
  6. Compare online and via broker same week. Market moves. Get online quote from Hiscox and AXA, then ask broker for Aviva/ QBE/ Zurich alternative with same inputs. Same turnover, same date within 7 days to make comparison fair. Document observed date.

FAQ: Professional Indemnity Insurance Quote UK

What is a professional indemnity insurance quote?

A quote is an insurer's estimate for PI cover based on your profession, turnover, limit, claims history, and jurisdiction. It is VARIABLE, not guaranteed premium. You get it online or via broker, usually valid 14-30 days, and it becomes policy once you pay and receive schedule and wording.

How much does professional indemnity insurance cost in the UK per month?

Illustrative range observed August 2026: £8–£45 per month for £250k–£1m cover for low-risk consultants, sole traders, contractors. IT, design, construction pay more — £25–£200/month illustrative for £1m–£2m. Cost is VARIABLE by turnover, profession, limit, and claims. Check official insurer site for live quote.

Is professional indemnity insurance legally required in the UK?

No single UK law requires all businesses to hold PI, unlike Employers' Liability under the Employers' Liability (Compulsory Insurance) Act 1969. However, regulators such as FCA for advisers, RIBA for architects, RICS for surveyors, and many client contracts require it. You must check your professional body and contract terms.

What limit do I need for a professional indemnity quote?

Check your largest client contract. Many UK contracts ask £1m any one claim. Freelancers with low-value advice often choose £250k–£500k. Architects and surveyors may need £2m–£5m. Limit affects cost significantly. If unsure, ask your broker and consider aggregate vs any one claim.

Does professional indemnity cover past work?

Only if you have retroactive cover. Most UK PI is claims-made with a retroactive date. If retroactive is unlimited or back to when you started, past work is covered if claim is made today and you had continuous cover. If you let cover lapse, you need run-off. Check your schedule.

Can I get PI insurance as a sole trader or contractor outside IR35?

Yes. Sole traders, limited company contractors, partnerships, and LLPs can all get PI. Sole traders are often cheapest online via Simply Business or Hiscox, illustrative £6–£20/month for £250k. Your employment status for IR35 does not remove need for PI. Your personal assets are at risk if uninsured.

What documents do I need to get a fast PI quote?

Have ready: profession description, qualifications, annual turnover last and next year, % overseas work, largest single contract value, claims in last 5 years with amounts, retroactive date, required limit, and whether you need USA/Canada. With these, you can get a binding quote in 10–15 minutes online.
